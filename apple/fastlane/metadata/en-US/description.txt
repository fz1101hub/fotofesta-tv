FotoFesta TV turns your Apple TV into a digital photo frame for parties, family dinners and evenings with friends.

WHERE YOUR PHOTOS COME FROM
• Guest photos: guests scan a QR code on the TV and send photos from their own phones, over your home network and with no app to install. New photos appear right away in the slideshow with the sender's name; if you like, you approve them first with the remote.
• Google Photos: scan a QR code with your phone, sign in and choose the photos to show. No passwords to type with the remote, and access only to the photos you select.
• iCloud Photos: albums from the Apple TV Photos library, including favorites and shared albums. Photos added to the album appear by themselves.
• Network folders: photos and videos from media servers (DLNA) on your home network, such as NAS drives, PCs, Plex and Jellyfin.
• Four demo photos to try the app right away.

SLIDESHOW
• 3 to 20 seconds per photo
• Fade, slide and gentle zoom transitions
• Slow pan and zoom (Ken Burns effect)
• Whole photo, fill screen or blurred sides, shuffle without repeats
• Mosaic: the current photo large, with the last ones shown beside it
• Countdown to a time you choose, for Midnight, Cake, Toast or Surprise: in the last seconds big numbers fill the screen, then a message appears
• Videos from Google Photos, iCloud Photos and network folders, played from start to finish in formats Apple TV supports, such as MP4 and MOV. Leave them out with the “Videos” setting, or mute them with “Video sound”
• Clock and sleep timer after 30, 60 or 120 minutes

PHOTO GAMES
• Find the pair: match identical photos
• Mystery photo: guess the blurred picture
• Puzzle: put the photo back together, in three levels: Easy (6 pieces), Medium (12) and Hard (24)
• Who took it? Guess which guest sent the photo. Needs party photos from at least two named guests

FOR THE PARTY
• Answer on your phone: in “Mystery photo” and “Who took it?” guests answer from their own phones, on the same party page they use to send photos, with no app. The TV shows how many have answered, then reveals the answer and the leaderboard.
• Party awards: at the end come the Golden lens for the guest who sent the most photos, the first photo of the party and the Grand finale, the last one.

PRIVACY
No ads, no analytics and no developer server. Selected photos are shown straight from Google Photos and never copied to your Apple TV, and “Disconnect and delete” revokes access. Guest photos go from the phone to the Apple TV without passing through the internet and are deleted when the party ends. iCloud Photos and network folders are read on the fly, with no copies.

Google Photos doesn't let apps read your whole library: you choose the photos from your phone each time.
