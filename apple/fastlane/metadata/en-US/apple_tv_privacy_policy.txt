FotoFesta TV shows on your TV the photos you choose. It contains no ads, analytics or tracking and sends no data to the developer.

Google Photos (optional): the Apple TV talks directly to Google over encrypted connections. The app only requests access to the photos you pick on your phone, not to the rest of your library. Picked photos are not copied to the Apple TV: they load from Google while they are shown and are kept only in memory. The Apple TV keeps the list of chosen photos and the Google access token, stored in the Keychain.

Settings › "Disconnect and delete" removes the list of photos and the token, closes the selection at Google and revokes access. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Google keeps a selection available for about a week.

The full privacy policy is available at the privacy policy URL of this app and as a QR code in the app's Settings.
