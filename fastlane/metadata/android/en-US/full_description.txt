FotoFesta TV turns your television into a digital photo frame for parties, family dinners and evenings with friends.

<b>Where your photos come from</b>
• Google Photos: scan a QR code with your phone, sign in and choose the photos to show. No passwords to type with the remote, and access only to the photos you select.
• USB drive or stick: choose a folder, even without internet. Photos stay on the drive and are never copied.
• Four demo photos to try the app right away.

<b>Slideshow</b>
• 3 to 20 seconds per photo
• Fade, slide and gentle zoom transitions
• Slow pan and zoom (Ken Burns effect)
• Whole photo or fill screen, shuffle without repeats
• Clock and background music from a track on your TV
• Sleep timer after 30, 60 or 120 minutes
• Screensaver with your photos when the TV is idle

<b>Photo games</b>
• Memory match: find the pairs
• Mystery photo: guess the blurred picture

<b>Built for the remote</b>
Everything works with the remote's arrow keys, with a clearly visible focus, text readable from the couch and an option to reduce motion.

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, no tracking by the app and no developer server. Google photos are shown straight from Google Photos and never copied to the TV, so they take no storage space.

Google Photos doesn't let apps read your whole library: you choose the photos from your phone each time.
