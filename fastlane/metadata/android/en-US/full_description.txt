FotoFesta TV turns your television into a digital photo frame for parties, family dinners and evenings with friends.

<b>Where your photos come from</b>
• Google Photos: scan a QR code with your phone, sign in and choose the photos to show. No passwords to type with the remote, and access only to the photos you select.
• Guest photos: guests scan a QR code on the TV and send photos from their own phones, over your home network and with no app to install. New photos appear right away in the slideshow with the sender's name; if you like, you approve them first with the remote.
• USB drive or stick: choose a folder, even without internet. Photos and videos stay on the drive and are never copied.
• Home network: shared folders on PCs, Macs and NAS drives (SMB, with user name and password or as a guest) and DLNA media servers on NAS drives, routers, Plex and Jellyfin. Browse folders with the remote; photos and videos load on the fly, with no copies on the TV and nothing going through the internet.
• iCloud shared albums: turn on “Public Website” on iPhone, scan the QR code on the TV and paste the link on your phone. Photos added to the album appear by themselves.
• Four demo photos to try the app right away.

<b>Slideshow</b>
• 3 to 20 seconds per photo
• Fade, slide and gentle zoom transitions
• Slow pan and zoom (Ken Burns effect)
• Whole photo, fill screen or blurred sides, shuffle without repeats
• Mosaic: the current photo large, with the last ones shown beside it
• Countdown to a time you choose, for Midnight, Cake, Toast or Surprise: in the last seconds big numbers fill the screen, then a message appears
• Clock and background music: a single track or a whole folder as a playlist
• Sleep timer after 30, 60 or 120 minutes
• Screensaver with your photos when the TV is idle

<b>Videos too</b>
• Videos from USB, your home network, iCloud and Google Photos join the slideshow and play from start to finish. Prefer photos only? Turn them off with the “Videos” setting.
• Video sound: while a video plays its sound, the background music pauses.
• Many formats: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI and more. Which codecs play depends on your TV; a video it can't play is skipped.
• Games and the screensaver use photos only.

<b>Photo games</b>
• Find the pair: match identical photos
• Mystery photo: guess the blurred picture
• Puzzle: put the photo back together, in three levels: Easy (6 pieces), Medium (12) and Hard (24)
• Who took it? Guess which guest sent the photo. Needs party photos from at least two named guests

<b>For the party</b>
• Answer on your phone: in “Mystery photo” and “Who took it?” guests answer from their own phones, on the same party page they use to send photos, with no app. The TV shows how many have answered, then reveals the answer and the leaderboard.
• Party awards: at the end come the Golden lens for the guest who sent the most photos, the first photo of the party and the Grand finale, the last one.

<b>Built for the remote</b>
Everything works with the remote's arrow keys, with a clearly visible focus, text readable from the couch and an option to reduce motion.

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, no tracking and no developer server. Google photos are shown straight from Google Photos and never copied to the TV, so they take no storage space. Guest photos go from the phone to the TV without passing through the internet, stay in memory during the party and are deleted at the end.

Google Photos doesn't let apps read your whole library: you choose the photos from your phone each time.
